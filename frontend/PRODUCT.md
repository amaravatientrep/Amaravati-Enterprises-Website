## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind, hosted on Vercel, repo `amaravatientrep/Amaravati-Enterprises-Website`. The redesign is built on the `redesign` branch and reviewed on a Vercel preview URL; the live domain (www.amaravatienterprises.com, served from `main`) is not touched until the owner approves.

## Users

Primary: owners and purchase managers of independent 3–4★ hotels and local hotel chains all over India. They restock guest-room amenities regularly, compare suppliers on quality, price and reliability, and usually reach out by phone or WhatsApp. They often browse on a phone.

Secondary (served, not the lead audience): branded chain properties, institutions and hospitals (patient admission kits), corporate guest houses.

## Product Purpose

Amaravati Enterprises is a Hyderabad supplier of hotel guest amenities with custom hotel branding. The website exists to turn a visiting hotel buyer into an enquiry (quote form, WhatsApp, call) and to rank on Google for hotel-amenity searches in Hyderabad and all over India. Success = more qualified enquiries from hotels.

## Positioning

A nearby, one-stop hotel-amenity partner for hotels all over India: the full guest-room range (soaps, toiletries, amenity kits, printed packaging) with the hotel's own name and branding on it, backed by a real roster of hotel clients and two partner brands, Biotique (hotel toiletries) and Diversey (housekeeping/cleaning).

Owner instruction: do NOT emphasise manufacturing. Some products are made in-house but capacity is limited; never lead with "manufacturer", "factory" or "made in our unit". At most a quiet mention on the About page. Lead with range, custom branding, local supply, and clients.

## Product Principles

1. Every page leads to an enquiry that actually reaches the owner: WhatsApp first, form second, call third.
2. Show proof, don't claim it: real client logos, real numbers, real product photos, a real address.
3. Local partner advantage: a Hyderabad supplier serving all over India with custom branding is the core story (not manufacturing).
4. Fast on Indian mobile data: buyers browse on phones; pages must load quickly on 4G.
5. Be findable: dedicated, indexable pages per product category and for Hyderabad/Telangana/AP searches.
